How School Dashboard Uses Google

We want you to know exactly what happens when you connect your Google account, so here is the longer version in plain English.

Why does School Dashboard use Google?

Some of School Dashboard's most useful features work with information from your Google account. Signing in with Google lets the dashboard connect to the Google services needed for those features.

Google handles the sign-in and permission screen. Before access is granted, Google shows you the permissions requested by the app.

What Google information can be used?

The exact information available depends on the Google permissions enabled for the app and the features you use. The current application is designed to work with these Google services:

Google Classroom

Classroom information can be used to show courses, coursework, student submissions, and related schoolwork in the dashboard.

Google Calendar

Calendar access can be used to display upcoming events and help bring your schedule into one place.

Google Drive

Drive access can be used by dashboard features that search for recent or starred files and manage starred state.

Important: Google only provides information covered by the permissions you approve. If a feature is not connected to Google, it does not need access to your Google account.

What about AI?

School Dashboard has optional AI-powered features in both Source Citation and the Assignment Tracker.

Source Citation

The Source Citation tool can use Gemini to improve or fill in citation information when the normal webpage or book lookup does not provide enough metadata.

Assignment Scanner

The Assignment Tracker can optionally accept a picture of written or printed assignments. The image is sent through the server to Gemini so it can identify assignment titles, courses, due dates, and visible notes.

The Assignment Scanner does not save the uploaded image to the School Dashboard server, a database, browser local storage, or the tracker. The image is processed temporarily for the request and then discarded by the feature. Only assignments you choose to add are saved locally as normal tracker data.

These AI features are optional. They do not give Gemini access to your Google Classroom, Calendar, or Drive account. The image scanner is not a general-purpose reader of your Google account.

Other services used for citations

Google Books

Book citation features can use Google Books to look up bibliographic information such as book titles, authors, and editions.

Open Library

If the primary book lookup does not find enough information, the citation tool can use Open Library as a fallback.

These citation services are separate from your Google account sign-in permissions.

Do we sell your information?

No. School Dashboard is not designed to sell your personal information or Google account information. Google account access is used to provide the features you choose to use.

For the complete details about information handling, please see our Privacy Policy.

Can I use School Dashboard without Google?

Yes, where a feature does not require Google account data. School Dashboard includes tools that can be used without signing in. Google-powered features, such as loading your Classroom or Calendar information, naturally require the appropriate Google connection.
