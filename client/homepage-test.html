What is School Dashboard?

School Dashboard is a student-focused organizer designed to put commonly used school resources in one simple location.

Google Classroom

Connect your Google account to access the Classroom information available to the app.

Assignments

Keep school assignments organized and use tools designed to make schoolwork easier to manage.

Schedule

View school scheduling information in one place when the required Google services are connected.

Citations

Create and organize source citations for school projects and research.

Study Tools

Use additional tools designed to help with studying, organization, and school projects.

One Place

Access your school resources from a single dashboard instead of keeping everything in separate places.

How Google is Used

School Dashboard uses Google services to provide features that work with your school account. Depending on the features you use and the permissions you approve, this can include accessing information from services such as Google Classroom.

When you choose to sign in with Google, Google shows you the permissions requested by School Dashboard. You can review those permissions before granting access.

The app only requests access needed for the Google-powered features that are available. You can use the parts of School Dashboard that do not require a Google account without signing in.

Privacy & Security

We want you to understand what School Dashboard accesses and why. Our privacy policy explains how information is handled, while our terms describe the rules for using the service.
